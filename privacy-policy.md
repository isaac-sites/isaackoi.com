# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Isaac Koi properties, including subdomains of isaackoi.com.

## Information we collect

When you visit our sites, basic technical information may be processed by our hosting, security, analytics, and performance providers. This can include pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, referral source, time of visit, and similar usage information. If you contact us, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, and message.

## Analytics and improvement

We may use tools such as Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Cloudflare analytics, or similar privacy-conscious services to understand which pages are useful, how visitors find the site, and whether pages are working properly. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies depending on how they are configured. We do not use analytics to identify individual visitors personally.

## Affiliate links and advertising

Some pages may include affiliate links, advertising links, or product references. If you click a link and make a purchase, Isaac Koi may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate partners may use their own cookies or tracking methods to record qualifying visits or purchases.

## Your choices

You can control cookies and tracking technologies through your browser settings, privacy extensions, and the settings offered by third-party services. You can also avoid clicking affiliate or advertising links if you prefer not to leave the site through those links.

## Contact

If you have a privacy question or would like to ask about information you have sent to us, please use the contact page.
