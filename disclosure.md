# Affiliate & AI Disclosure

Please check important facts against original and authoritative sources before relying on them, especially for health, financial, legal, or safety decisions.

## Editorial independence

Isaac Koi aims to present useful and relevant information, but AI assistance and commercial availability should not be treated as personal expertise, testing of a product, or a recommendation suited to your circumstances.
